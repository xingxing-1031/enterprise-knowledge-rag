# Supplier Onboarding Process

## Required registration

A new supplier must provide its legal name, tax registration, bank account, contact, and signed compliance declaration before onboarding.

## Review and activation

Procurement checks the registration package and at least two quotations when the planned purchase exceeds 30,000 yuan. A procurement administrator activates the supplier after review.
