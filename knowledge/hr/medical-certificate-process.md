# Medical Certificate and Emergency Submission Process

## Certificate requirements

Sick leave longer than two working days requires a certificate issued by a medical institution. The certificate must identify the employee and the covered dates.

## Emergency submission

When an emergency prevents advance submission, notify the direct manager before the workday starts. Submit the system request and supporting certificate within two working days after returning.
